Safety & travel tips

A few reminders to help you enjoy a comfortable visit around Kanyakumari.

🧭

General safety

Keep important belongings secure, avoid isolated places late at night, and follow local guidance.

🌊

At the coast

Follow local beach and sea-safety notices. Avoid restricted areas and take care during rough conditions.

☀️

Weather & comfort

Carry drinking water, protect yourself from the sun, and check local weather before outdoor plans.

📱

Stay connected

Keep your phone charged and share your travel plans with someone you trust.
